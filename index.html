Quest 01 · Legend Rookie

What Is a Narrative Text?

A narrative text tells a story. It usually has characters, a setting, a problem, and a solution.

Teks naratif menceritakan sebuah kisah. Biasanya terdapat tokoh, latar, masalah, dan penyelesaian.

Explore the Story Elements

Characters — the people or beings in a story.

Example: Dayang Sumbi, Sangkuriang, and Tumang.

Setting — where and when a story happens.

Example: West Java, long ago.

Problem — the main conflict.

Example: Sangkuriang wants to marry Dayang Sumbi without knowing she is his mother.

Solution — how the conflict ends.

Example: Dayang Sumbi gives him an impossible task, and the boat is flipped.

Quest 02 · Traces of a Legend

What Is a Legend?

A legend is a traditional story connected to a place or history. It may include magical events and teach a lesson.

Legenda adalah cerita tradisional yang berkaitan dengan suatu tempat atau sejarah. Ceritanya dapat memuat peristiwa ajaib dan memberikan pelajaran.

Characteristics of a Legend

⌛ Connected to history

◷ Set in the past

♟ Human characters

✧ Magical elements

❖ Passed down through generations

◈ Linked to a place

▲ May explain a place’s origin

✦ Contains a moral message

Mini-game · Legend or Not?

Decide whether each statement describes a legend.

1. A legend may explain the origin of a real place.

Correct. Legends often explain the origin of places, such as Mount Tangkuban Perahu.

2. A legend must take place in the future with modern robots.

Correct. Legends are usually set in the past, not necessarily in a future with robots.

3. A legend can be passed down through generations.

Correct. Legends are passed from one generation to the next.

Quest 04 · Storybook

The Legend of Tangkuban Perahu

Read all five scenes in English. Listen to the audio or open an Indonesian translation only when you need it.

01 / 05 · ORIENTATION

The Beginning

Once upon a time in West Java, Dayang Sumbi lived with her son Sangkuriang. One day, her weaving tool fell. She promised to marry whoever retrieved it. Tumang brought it back, and she married him.

Pada zaman dahulu di Jawa Barat, Dayang Sumbi tinggal bersama putranya, Sangkuriang. Suatu hari, alat tenunnya jatuh. Ia berjanji akan menikahi siapa pun yang mengambilnya. Tumang mengambilnya, dan ia pun menikah dengannya.

02 / 05 · COMPLICATION

The Conflict Begins

Sangkuriang went hunting with Tumang, unaware that the dog was his father. In anger, he killed Tumang. Dayang Sumbi was shocked and asked him to leave.

Sangkuriang pergi berburu bersama Tumang tanpa mengetahui bahwa anjing itu adalah ayahnya. Karena marah, ia membunuh Tumang. Dayang Sumbi terkejut dan menyuruhnya pergi.

03 / 05 · COMPLICATION

Years Later

Years later, Sangkuriang returned as an adult. They did not recognize each other. He fell in love with Dayang Sumbi and wanted to marry her. Dayang Sumbi realized that he was her son and refused.

Bertahun-tahun kemudian, Sangkuriang kembali sebagai orang dewasa. Awalnya mereka tidak saling mengenali. Ia jatuh cinta kepada Dayang Sumbi dan ingin menikahinya. Dayang Sumbi menyadari bahwa ia adalah putranya dan menolak.

04 / 05 · COMPLICATION

The Impossible Task

Dayang Sumbi asked Sangkuriang to build a big boat and a lake in one night. He almost finished the task with magic, but she tricked him by making it look like dawn.

Dayang Sumbi meminta Sangkuriang membuat perahu besar dan danau dalam satu malam. Dengan bantuan kekuatan magis, ia hampir berhasil, tetapi Dayang Sumbi membuat suasana tampak seperti fajar.

05 / 05 · RESOLUTION

The Flipped Boat

Sangkuriang realized that he had been deceived and became angry. He kicked the unfinished boat until it flipped. According to the legend, the upside-down boat became Mount Tangkuban Perahu.

Sangkuriang sadar bahwa ia telah diperdaya dan menjadi marah. Ia menendang perahu yang belum selesai hingga terbalik. Menurut legenda, perahu terbalik itu menjadi Gunung Tangkuban Perahu.

Quest 05 · The Story’s Secrets

Story Structure, Language & Moral

Orientation

Introduces the characters and setting: Dayang Sumbi, Sangkuriang, Tumang, and West Java.

Complication

The conflict grows: Tumang dies, Sangkuriang returns, and an impossible task is set.

Resolution

The boat is kicked upside down, explaining the mountain’s shape in the legend.

Orientasi mengenalkan tokoh dan latar. Komplikasi mengembangkan masalah. Resolusi menceritakan akhir konflik ketika perahu terbalik.

Language Features

Past tense: lived, went, returned, kicked.

Time expressions: once upon a time, one day, years later.

Action verbs: brought, built, kicked.

Specific characters and place: Sangkuriang, Dayang Sumbi, West Java.

Ciri bahasa meliputi kata kerja lampau, penanda waktu, kata kerja tindakan, serta nama tokoh dan tempat tertentu.

Moral Value

Think before you act. Anger can lead to harmful consequences.

Berpikirlah sebelum bertindak. Kemarahan dapat membawa akibat buruk.
